ThreeTerm libslvs worker corresponding-source offer

For each recipient who receives the ThreeTerm libslvs worker in object-code
form, ThreeTerm offers the complete Corresponding Source for the worker,
including the pinned SolveSpace libslvs source and the scripts needed to build
the worker, at no charge other than the reasonable cost of delivery.

This written offer is valid for at least three years from the date the worker
artifact is distributed, and for as long as ThreeTerm offers support for that
artifact. The Corresponding Source is available from the immutable source
commit:
https://github.com/solvespace/solvespace/tree/27b6a080c8b669421bd4d444650c3b8eddec5687

The ThreeTerm worker source and build scripts are distributed with the source
release at the same revision as the artifact and are maintained at:
https://github.com/rafaelromao/threeterm
