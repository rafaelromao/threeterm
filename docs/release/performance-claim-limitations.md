# Performance Claim Limitations

Admitted performance claims are limited to the named L-bracket rehearsal
fixture, the declared project scale, the pinned native-worker container, and
the direct local Ghostty renderer and terminal topology recorded by the signed
six-gate performance record. They are not product-wide guarantees.

Cold-start, compositor, input-to-photon, human-usability, cross-platform, and
unexercised project-scale axes remain outside the claim unless a later signed
record names and measures them.
